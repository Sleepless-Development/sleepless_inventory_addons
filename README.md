## Modules

### backItems

Shows weapons and other configured items on the player while they are in the inventory. Slot groups, priority, and attachment offsets are set in `backItems/config.lua`. Attached items hide in most vehicles and while noclip is on.

### dragCraft

Craft by dragging one inventory item onto another. Recipes in `dragCraft/config.lua` name the two ingredients, how long the craft takes, and what it produces. A result can be a fixed amount or a random range, and client or server callbacks can cancel the craft.

### itemCarry

Plays a carry animation and attaches a prop while the player holds one configured item, such as a box or a TV. A second carry item is refused. If one is created while they are already carrying, it is removed and dropped on the ground. Items are set in `itemCarry/config.lua`.
